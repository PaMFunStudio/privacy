Privacy Policy

App: PaMVideoCut Pro

Last updated: 2026-02-09

1. General Information

This policy outlines the data processing rules for the "PaMVideoCut Pro" application. We respect your privacy and ensure your data security.

2. Video Processing

All video editing, rendering, and merging operations are performed locally on your device (using FFmpeg technology). Your video files are never uploaded to our servers or shared with any third parties.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app displays advertisements via Google AdMob. Therefore:

  • Google may collect advertising identifiers (e.g., AAID) to personalize the content shown.
  • This data is used to analyze ad effectiveness and provide more relevant offers.
  • You can manage your ad privacy settings directly in your operating system's settings.

4. Settings and Session Storage

The app remembers your preferences to improve your experience:

  • Local Storage: Quality settings (bitrate) and trimming parameters are stored on the device to ensure workflow continuity.
