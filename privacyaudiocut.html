Privacy Policy

App: PaMAudioCut Pro

Last updated: 2026-02-09

1. General Information

This policy outlines the data processing rules for the "PaMAudioCut Pro" application. We respect your privacy and ensure your data security.

2. Audio Processing

All audio editing, trimming, and conversion (Mono/Stereo) operations are performed locally on your device using the FFmpeg engine. Your audio files are never uploaded to our servers or shared with any third parties.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app displays advertisements via Google AdMob. Therefore:

  • Google may collect advertising identifiers (e.g., AAID) to personalize the content shown.
  • This data is used to analyze ad effectiveness.
  • You can manage your ad privacy settings directly in your operating system's settings.

4. Personalization and Settings

The app remembers your preferences to improve your experience:

  • Technical Parameters: Bitrate and channel mode settings (Mono/Stereo) are stored locally on the device.
